Charts

templates[] · charts[]

templates is an array. Each template wraps a bundle of charts. Below: one full template (tragedy) rendered field-by-field — its top-level keys first, then its two chart definitions indented below. Notice how chart fields like id, y_items, and label light up differently — they're persisted, immutable, or user-facing.
